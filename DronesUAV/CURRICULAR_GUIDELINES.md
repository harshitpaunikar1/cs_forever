## Intent

This UAV roadmap is structured to prioritize the open-source tools and workflows that show up most often in serious drone software, autonomy, simulation, and embedded flight-stack work.

## Practical Reading For This Local Copy

- Foundation builds Linux, Git, C++, Python, aerodynamics intuition, and flight-stack awareness.
- Core UAV covers autopilot architecture, MAVLink, QGroundControl, ROS 2, estimation, control, and perception.
- Advanced extends into NuttX, JSBSim, multi-vehicle coordination, logs, reliability, and hiring readiness.
- Projects convert theory into buildable UAV systems work.
