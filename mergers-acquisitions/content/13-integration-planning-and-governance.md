# Integration Planning and Governance

## Overview

Integration planning is the roadmap for bringing two companies together after closing. Governance means setting up the leadership structure (steering committee, integration management office, workstream leads) that decides and tracks every integration action.

---

## Why It Matters

Most of the value in an M&A deal is won or lost in the first 100 days after closing. Strong planning and clear decision rights keep operations running, retain key people, and deliver the synergies that justified the price.

## Key Principles

- Start integration planning during diligence, not after signing
- Set up an Integration Management Office (IMO) with a full-time lead
- Define day-one readiness and 100-day priorities
- Assign clear owners, milestones, and KPIs for every workstream
- Communicate early and often to employees, customers, and suppliers

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **IMO** | Integration Management Office that runs the integration |
| **Day One** | First day after closing when the combined entity operates |
| **100-Day Plan** | Priority actions for the first 100 days |
| **Steering Committee** | Senior group that makes key integration decisions |
| **Workstream** | Functional track (HR, IT, Finance) of integration work |

## Use Case

A buyer sets up an IMO led by a seasoned program manager, nine functional workstreams, weekly steering-committee reviews, and a published 100-day plan shared with the combined leadership team on day one.

## Scenario

> A buyer in the insurance industry stood up its IMO two months before closing. On day one, all customer-service phone lines were combined, pay slips went out on time, and 92% of top-100 customers received a personal call from new account managers.

## Examples

- A buyer names a Chief Integration Officer for a $2B cross-border deal.
- A PE firm uses a 100-day value creation plan focused on three EBITDA levers.

## 1. Topic Snapshot
Integration planning is the roadmap and decision architecture for combining two companies post-close; governance is the steering-committee + IMO + workstream-lead structure that makes and tracks every integration call. Most M&A value is won or lost in the first 100 days, so any IT/AI/Product/Consulting leader running or advising post-merger integration must install the IMO scaffolding during diligence, not after signing. The decision this framework answers: *what must be true on Day 1, Day 30, Day 100, and Day 365 for this deal to hit its synergy thesis without breaking the run-rate business?*

## 3. Frameworks & Matrices

### 3.1 IMO Operating Model
**Purpose:** Define who decides what during integration.
```
                +---------------------------+
                |    Steering Committee      |
                |  (CEO, CFO, BU Heads, GC)  |
                +-------------+--------------+
                              |
                    +---------v---------+
                    |  CIO-Integ / IMO  |
                    |       Lead        |
                    +---------+---------+
                              |
   +-----------+-----------+--+--+-----------+-----------+
   |           |           |     |           |           |
  [HR]       [IT]      [Finance][Sales]   [Product]   [Legal]
   |           |           |     |           |           |
  Sub      Sub-teams     Sub-   Sub-      Sub-        Sub-
  teams   (Cutover,      teams  teams     teams       teams
          AD, Apps,
          Data)
```
**Components:** Steerco (weekly), IMO (daily stand-up), workstream leads (functional), synergy tracker (horizontal), PMO tooling (Smartsheet/Jira).
**IT/AI/Product example:** AI-SaaS buyer dedicates a full-time IMO lead ex-McKinsey PM + 9 workstream leads; IT workstream split into Identity/Apps/Data/AI-Platform sub-teams.
**Trigger:** Signing + 0 days. Stand up before announcement.

### 3.2 Day-One / 100-Day Gantt
**Purpose:** Sequence the non-negotiables.
```
         Sign  Close(D1)  D30   D60   D100   D180   D365
          |       |        |     |      |      |      |
HR        |=======|>Offers>|>====|>====>|>Comp-harmonize>|
IT        |=======|>Cutover|>Apps-rationalize|>Data-merge>|
Finance   |       |>D1 GL  |>Close cycle|>ERP consolidate>|
Sales     |       |>Account map|>Cross-sell play|>Quota merge>|
Product   |       |>Roadmap freeze|>Feature-flag gov|>Unified>|
Legal     |=======|>Entity|>Contracts novation|>IP transfer>|
Customer  |       |>Letter|>Personal calls top-100|>NPS>|
Synergy   |       |       |>Capture tracker goes live>|>>>>|
```
**Components:** Milestones per workstream; critical path marked in red; dependencies between HR<->IT (AD), Finance<->IT (ERP), Legal<->all.
**Consulting example:** PE firm rolls 3 EBITDA levers (pricing, procurement, SG&A) onto this Gantt with $ targets per month.
**Trigger:** LOI to signing. Gantt is a deal-approval artifact.

### 3.3 Workstream Coverage Matrix
**Purpose:** Force completeness; no orphan function.

| Workstream | Day-1 Must | 100-Day Target | 12-Month Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR | Offers sent, payroll continuity, org chart L1-L3 | Comp harmonization, retention bonuses paid | Full HRIS merge, single performance cycle |
| IT | Email/AD federation, laptops, VPN | App rationalization plan, security posture equalized | ERP/CRM consolidation |
| Finance | Opening balance sheet, GL mapping, chart of accounts | First combined close | Single ERP, unified FP&A |
| Sales | Account ownership map, no-poach rules | Combined pipeline, cross-sell plays | Single CRM, unified comp plan |
| Product | Roadmap freeze or merge, feature-flag policy | Unified roadmap announced | Single codebase or deprecation path |
| Legal | Entity structure, contracts assignment | Novation of top contracts | IP/data migration complete |
| Ops/Supply | Supplier notifications, continuity letters | Top-supplier consolidation | Network optimization |
| GTM/Marketing | Brand guardrails, Day-1 comms | Joint positioning, website | Rebrand decisions executed |
| Synergy | Baseline locked | Tracker live, first capture | >50% run-rate capture |

**AI/Product example:** AI-SaaS buyer flags model-weights + training-data as extra Product sub-workstream (not in standard playbook).
**Trigger:** First IMO session; rerun at each stage gate.

### 3.4 Integration Operating-Model Decision Tree (Haspeslagh & Jemison 1991)
**Purpose:** Pick integration archetype; prevents over- or under-integration.
```
                     How much STRATEGIC INTERDEPENDENCE is needed?
                              Low                      High
                         +---------------+---------------+
How much ORGANIZATIONAL  |               |               |
AUTONOMY does target     |   PRESERVE    |   SYMBIOTIC   |
need?   High             |  (LVMH model) | (Disney+Pixar)|
                         +---------------+---------------+
                         |               |               |
                         |   HOLDING     |    ABSORB     |
                         Low             |  (Cisco SMB)  |
                         +---------------+---------------+
                                         + TRANSFORMATION (rare; both sides change)
```
**Components:** Autonomy axis (leave alone vs. consolidate), Interdependence axis (need shared resources).
**IT/AI example:** AI-SaaS target with unique research culture -> Preserve for 90 days (retain founders + lab autonomy), then Symbiotic as platform integrates.
**Consulting example:** IT-services roll-up -> Absorb (one ERP, one brand, one delivery model) within 100 days.
**Trigger:** Diligence phase; revisit at signing and Day 100.

## 4. Formulas

### 4.1 Integration Readiness Score (weighted)
```
IRS = 0.25*IT_ready + 0.20*HR_offers_sent + 0.15*TSA_signed
    + 0.15*Customer_comms_sent + 0.10*Supplier_contracts_OK
    + 0.15*Legal_entity_ready
```
Each sub-metric 0-100. Thresholds: **Go =95+, Conditional Go =85-94, Hold <85.**
**Example (AI-SaaS buyer integrating 200-person target):**
- Day 30 score: IT 70, HR 90, TSA 100, Cust 60, Supplier 80, Legal 100 -> IRS = 0.25*70 + 0.20*90 + 0.15*100 + 0.15*60 + 0.10*80 + 0.15*100 = **82** (Hold).
- Day 60: 85, 95, 100, 85, 90, 100 -> **91.25** (Conditional).
- Day 90: 95, 100, 100, 95, 95, 100 -> **97.5** (Go).

### 4.2 Day-One Readiness Gate (binary)
```
DayOneReady = AND(payroll_runs, email_works, customer_phones_answer,
                  legal_entity_live, bank_accounts_active,
                  top10_customer_letter_sent, exec_comms_delivered,
                  security_badge_access, executive_signatures_in_place)
```
All 9 = TRUE, else **no-go**. No partial credit. Rehearsed via tabletop exercise 2 weeks before close.

### 4.3 Synergy Capture Rate
```
SCR(month t) = Realized_synergy_run_rate(t) / Target_synergy_run_rate(t)
```
Targets: Month 3 =20%, Month 6 =50%, Month 12 =90%, Month 18 =100%.
**Example:** $50M target synergies. Month 6 actual run-rate $22M -> SCR = 44% (red; below 50% target).

### 4.4 Key-Talent Retention Rate
```
KTRR = Critical_talent_still_employed(t) / Critical_talent_at_close
```
**Target:** >=95% at 6 mo, >=90% at 12 mo, >=80% at 24 mo.
**Example:** 40 named critical talents at close. At 12 mo, 37 remain -> 92.5% (pass). If 34 -> 85% (red; triggers retention intervention).

## 5. Do vs Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Stand up IMO during diligence; appoint CIO-Integ pre-signing | Wait until after close; scramble with no leader |
| Dedicate full-time IMO lead and workstream leads | Run integration as side-of-desk for executives |
| Define Day-1 readiness checklist + tabletop rehearsal 2 weeks out | Discover broken payroll on Day 2 |
| Use clean room for competitively sensitive synergy planning pre-close | Share customer price lists in open data room pre-close (antitrust risk) |
| Pick one Haspeslagh-Jemison archetype and stick to it for 90 days | Oscillate between Preserve and Absorb every steerco |
| Lock synergy baseline; track run-rate monthly | Report only one-time savings; double-count with business-as-usual |
| Over-communicate (7x rule); cascade via managers | Single all-hands email then silence |
| Name top 20-40 key talents and execute retention plan week 1 | Lose the lead AI researcher at Month 3 because no stay bonus |
| Build reusable integration playbook; capture lessons at Day 100 and Day 365 | Re-invent on every deal |

## 6. Real-Life Scenarios

### Scenario 1 — AI-SaaS acquisition (Preserve -> Symbiotic)
Buyer (public SaaS, $3B rev) acquires 200-person vertical AI-SaaS target ($40M ARR) for $600M.
- **Archetype:** Preserve for 90 days, Symbiotic from Day 91.
- **IMO:** Full-time lead (ex-BCG), 9 workstreams; Product + AI-Platform get their own leads.
- **TSA:** Seller (former parent) provides finance + IT helpdesk for 6 months at $1.2M/mo; reverse TSA for 2 specialized data-labeling services.
- **Code-base merge:** Feature-flag governance policy - every shared feature behind a flag; progressive roll-out; dual-write data layer for 90 days; single-sign-on federated Day 1, full Okta merge Day 120.
- **AI-specific:** Model-weights inventory; training-data lineage audit (GDPR + copyright); MLOps pipeline harmonized on buyer's stack by Day 180.
- **Tools:** Jira for integration plan, Smartsheet Gantt, Slack shared-channel (#imo-daily), Confluence playbook, Culture Amp pulse survey weekly, Salesforce for top-100 customer outreach log.
- **Result:** 94% key-talent retention at 12 mo; 88% synergy capture by Month 18.

### Scenario 2 — IT-services roll-up (Absorb)
PE-backed IT-services platform acquires its 4th bolt-on, a 120-person MSP.
- **Archetype:** Absorb; one brand, one ERP (NetSuite), one PSA (ConnectWise), one delivery model.
- **100-day plan:** HR on buyer's HRIS by Day 45; financials consolidated Day 60; brand sunset Day 90; customer migration to buyer's ticketing Day 75.
- **Synergy levers:** SG&A (-$3.2M), procurement (-$0.8M), pricing uplift (+$1.5M).
- **Tools:** Asana for program plan, Smartsheet Gantt, Teams workstream channels.
- **Result:** 100-day synergy run-rate 65% captured; 2 mid-managers lost unplanned (lesson fed into playbook v2).

### Scenario 3 — ANTI-EXAMPLE: No IMO, no CIO (integration chaos)
Mid-cap industrial acquires $180M-rev competitor. No IMO stood up; each function head "owns" their bit.
- **Day 30:** quarterly-close breaks - chart-of-accounts mismatch, 3 subsidiaries missed, CFO escalates.
- **Day 45:** Big-4 advisory fly-in, scope $1.8M for 8 weeks to fix close + retro-build an IMO.
- **Day 60:** 7 of top-20 named key talents leave (no retention plan); estimated replacement cost $4.2M + 14 months capability loss.
- **Day 90:** 2 top-10 customers churn citing "confused account coverage"; ARR impact -$6.5M.
- **Total quantified chaos cost:** ~$12.5M + delayed synergy capture worth another ~$8M NPV.
- **Root cause:** No single accountable leader; no Day-1 checklist; no stage gates; no RAID log; no communication cascade.

## 7. Implementation Playbook
1. **Appoint** CIO-Integ and full-time IMO lead at signing; publish RACI and steerco cadence within 5 days.
2. **Stand up** 9-workstream structure with named leads and executive sponsors; artifact = IMO charter + workstream charters in Confluence.
3. **Build** Day-1 readiness checklist and 100-day Gantt in Smartsheet; artifact = stage-gate document approved by steerco.
4. **Rehearse** Day-1 cutover via tabletop exercise 2 weeks pre-close; artifact = cutover runbook + rollback plan.
5. **Launch** synergy tracker with locked baseline; artifact = synergy ledger with owner, target, dependencies, monthly actuals.
6. **Execute** communication cascade (CEO video Day 1, manager talking-points, top-100 customer calls by Day 7); artifact = comms plan + pulse-survey dashboard.
7. **Run** weekly IMO stand-up + bi-weekly steerco with RAID log review; artifact = decision log + escalation tracker.
8. **Harvest** lessons at Day 100 and Day 365 retro; artifact = integration playbook v(n+1) checked into serial-acquirer library.

**Supplement with:**
1. Haspeslagh, P. & Jemison, D. - *Managing Acquisitions: Creating Value Through Corporate Renewal* (Free Press, 1991) - archetype model.
2. Galpin, T. & Herndon, M. - *The Complete Guide to Mergers and Acquisitions*, 3rd ed. (Jossey-Bass, 2014) - integration playbook + tools.

**Red flags:**
- Beware templatized 100-day plan applied to Preserve-archetype deals (kills the target's culture).
- Beware clean-room leakage pre-close (HSR / EU merger regulation).
- Beware single-threaded IMO lead with no exec air-cover -> decision latency kills momentum.
- Beware synergy baselines that drift (no locked starting point = no accountability).

## 9. Quick-Recall Card
- **IMO before signing**, not after; CIO-Integ named day of LOI.
- **Day 1 is binary**: payroll, email, phones, legal entity, comms - all green or delay.
- **100-day plan** = 9 workstreams x stage-gated milestones x synergy tracker.
- **Pick the archetype** (Preserve / Absorb / Symbiotic / Transformation) and commit.
- **Retention + communication** win or lose the deal before synergies ever do.
- As a PM/Consultant/AI Lead, the one question to answer with this framework is: *"What must be demonstrably true on Day 1, Day 100, and Day 365 - by workstream, with named owners and measurable gates - for this deal's synergy thesis to survive first contact with the operating reality?"*
