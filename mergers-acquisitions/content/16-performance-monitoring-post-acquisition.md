# Performance Monitoring Post-Acquisition

## Overview

Performance monitoring is the regular tracking of financial and operational results after a deal closes, compared to the plan that justified the acquisition. It keeps the board honest about whether the deal is working and triggers corrective action when it is not.

---

## Why It Matters

Without monitoring, synergies slip, costs creep back, and accountability fades. A disciplined review loop spots problems early, drives decisions, and creates lessons that improve future deals.

## Key Principles

- Set clear KPIs tied to the original deal rationale
- Compare actuals to the deal model, not just to budget
- Review monthly in the first year, quarterly after that
- Trigger a post-deal review 12 to 18 months after closing
- Capture lessons learned and feed them into the next deal

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Deal Model** | Financial model that supported the acquisition case |
| **Value Scorecard** | One-page view of synergies vs. plan |
| **Post-Acquisition Review** | Formal look-back to assess if goals were met |
| **Integration KPIs** | Metrics like retention, cross-sell rate, cost capture |
| **Course Correction** | Adjustments made when performance drifts from plan |

## Use Case

Twelve months after closing, the CFO presents a value scorecard showing revenue growth 3% ahead of plan, cost synergies on track, but IT integration slipping. The board releases extra funds to the IT workstream and resets its deadline.

## Scenario

> A PE firm reviewed a portfolio acquisition 18 months after closing. Revenue was 10% below plan but EBITDA was 5% above due to lower costs. The firm replaced the sales head, relaunched the product roadmap, and exited the deal 3 years later at 2.3x money.

## Examples

- A listed acquirer shares acquisition KPIs with analysts each quarter for 2 years.
- A PE fund holds a post-mortem on every deal to improve its next diligence checklist.

## 1. Topic Snapshot
Post-deal performance monitoring is the disciplined, multi-year tracking of a target's financial and operational results against the *deal model* (not just the annual budget) so synergies, retention and integration actually land in the consolidated P&L. The IT/AI/Product/Consulting leader must defend this tracking loop into the real GL, force monthly cadence in year one and quarterly thereafter, and hold the deal sponsor personally accountable 12–18 months after close when the honeymoon ends and value leakage begins. The decision: does the deal still earn its WACC, or do we course-correct (reallocate capital, replace leaders, restructure roadmap) or impair goodwill.

## 3. Frameworks & Matrices

### 3.1 Value Scorecard
**Purpose:** One page that every board member can read in 90 seconds, anchored to deal-model baseline.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| VALUE SCORECARD  — Target: AI-SaaS Co. | 12M post-close         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| STRATEGIC        | Customer retention  92%  (Plan 90%)   GREEN  |
|                  | Cross-sell ARR      $4.1M (Plan $5M)  AMBER  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| FINANCIAL        | Revenue vs Plan     +3%               GREEN  |
|                  | EBITDA vs Plan      -2%               AMBER  |
|                  | FCF vs Plan         -8%               AMBER  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| INTEGRATION      | IT cutover          65% (Plan 90%)    RED    |
|                  | Data migration      70%               AMBER  |
|                  | Synergy capture     88%               GREEN  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| PEOPLE           | Key-talent retention 85%              GREEN  |
|                  | eNPS                 +22              GREEN  |
|                  | Regretted attrition  7%               AMBER  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
```

**Components:** Strategic, Financial, Integration, People — each with ≤3 KPIs and RAG.
**Worked example:** AI-SaaS acquirer: revenue green, IT integration red → board releases extra $2M + resets go-live from Q2 to Q4.
**Trigger:** Any RED forces written remediation plan within 2 weeks.

### 3.2 Review Cadence Matrix

| Cadence | Who owns | Artefact | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly (Yr 1) | Target CFO | Flash pack + variance | Sponsor + PMO | Spot drift early |
| Quarterly (Yr 1–2) | Group CFO | Value scorecard | Board | Governance |
| Semi-annual | Integration Lead | VCP status | Ops committee | Rebaseline initiatives |
| Annual | Auditor | Goodwill test (IFRS 3 / ASC 805) | Audit committee | Impairment call |
| 18-Month Lookback | PE ops partner / Head of M&A | PAR memo | Investment committee | Lessons into next DD |

**Worked example:** Consulting firm rollup runs monthly CFO call + quarterly partner scorecard; PAR at 18 months feeds DD checklist v4.

### 3.3 Course-Correction Decision Tree

```
              Variance vs Deal Model
                      |
          +-----------+-----------+
          |                       |
     <5% (GREEN)             >=5% (AMBER/RED)
          |                       |
      Continue              Root-Cause Analysis
                                  |
                 +----------------+----------------+
                 |                |                |
           Commercial        Operational         People
          (pricing, churn)  (IT slip, COGS)   (leader gap)
                 |                |                |
         Reallocate spend   Restructure PMO   Replace leader
         + re-forecast      + reset timeline   + retention grants
                                  |
                         If >20% for 2 Qs:
                         Goodwill impairment test
```

**Trigger:** two consecutive quarters >10% shortfall OR any single >20% = mandatory IC escalation.

### 3.4 Goodwill Impairment Trigger Checklist (Optional)
- Sustained under-performance vs deal model (>2 quarters)
- Loss of anchor customer / top-3 engineer cluster
- Macro/sector multiple compression (>30%)
- Regulatory event (e.g., AI Act, data residency)
- Change in CGU structure post-reorg

## 4. Formulas

### 4.1 Synergy Capture Rate
`SCR = Realised Synergies / Committed Synergies`
Thresholds: ≥90% by month 24 = on-track; 70–90% = amber; <70% = red.
**Example (AI-SaaS):** committed $30M run-rate; realised $24M at M18 → SCR = 80% (amber) → reassign $3M to GTM re-acceleration.

### 4.2 Variance %
`Variance = (Actual − Plan) / Plan`
RAG: ±5% green, 5–10% amber, >10% red.
**Example:** Deal-model EBITDA $48M; actual 12M $44.2M → variance −7.9% (amber) → CFO must present remediation plan.

### 4.3 Post-Deal ROIC
`ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital`, compare to pre-deal WACC.
**Example:** NOPAT $85M; invested capital $900M → ROIC = 9.4%; WACC 11% → value-destructive → trigger 3.4 course correction.

### 4.4 Goodwill Impairment Indicator
`Impair if CGU Carrying Value > Recoverable Amount (= max(FVLCD, VIU))`
Under IFRS 3 / ASC 805, tested annually or on trigger.
**Example:** CGU carrying $620M; VIU (DCF of revised plan) $540M → impair $80M, disclose in 10-K/annual report.

### 4.5 EPS Accretion Realised
`EPS Accretion = EPS_combined − EPS_standalone_acquirer`
Validates the pitch made to analysts at announcement.

## 5. Do vs Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Freeze the deal-model baseline at close and compare against *it* | Quietly swap to annual budget so variances look smaller |
| Run monthly flash in Year 1, quarterly to board in Years 2–3 | Drop cadence to "as needed" after month 6 |
| Tie sponsor LTI/bonus to synergy capture + post-deal ROIC | Let deal sponsor rotate off before lookback |
| Run formal 18-month PAR and log lessons into DD playbook | Skip post-mortem because "everyone's moved on" |
| Disclose acquisition KPIs to analysts quarterly (listed cos) | Hide target inside segment so investors can't judge |
| Re-run goodwill impairment test on any trigger event | Wait for the statutory annual test only |
| Capture value leakage separately from macro drift | Blame everything on "the market" |

## 6. Real-Life Scenarios

### Scenario 1 — AI-SaaS Acquirer (Do-it-right)
Listed AI-SaaS firm acquires ML-ops target for $450M. CFO builds value scorecard; monthly CFO review + quarterly board pack for 24 months. At month 12: revenue +3% vs plan, cost synergies on track, IT integration at 65% (red). Board releases extra $2M and resets cutover to Q4. Quarterly KPIs shared with analysts for 2 years. Outcome: synergy capture 94% at month 24, multiple re-rates +1.5x.
**Tools:** Anaplan (plan vs actual), Power BI (scorecard), Workday (people KPIs), Confluence (PAR memo).

### Scenario 2 — Consulting Firm Rollup (Lookback discipline)
Mid-market consulting acquirer rolls up 4 boutiques over 3 years. Runs 18-month PAR per deal. Deal #2 revealed partner attrition drove 40% of value leakage → DD playbook v4 adds partner-lock-in clause + cultural-fit diagnostic. Deal #4 (post-playbook) hits 102% of plan. Lessons-learned log maintained in Notion with searchable tags.
**Tools:** Adaptive Planning, Looker, NotePlan/Notion, Tableau for partner-level P&L.

### Scenario 3 — ANTI-EXAMPLE (No cadence = impairment)
Industrials acquirer buys AI analytics firm for ₹1,800 cr. PMO disbands at month 6; cadence drops to annual budget review. No value scorecard, no PAR. At month 18, auditor forces goodwill impairment of ₹600 cr (33% of price) as CGU recoverable value slips below carrying value. Share price derates 18% on disclosure (~₹4,200 cr market cap wiped). CFO exits within a quarter; Big Four audit letter cites "absence of post-acquisition monitoring framework."
**Cost quantified:** ₹600 cr impairment + ₹4,200 cr market-cap loss + CFO replacement cost.

## 7. Implementation Playbook

1. **Freeze** the deal-model baseline in a locked workbook (CFO vault) within 2 weeks of close.
2. **Publish** a Value Scorecard template (Strategic / Financial / Integration / People) in Power BI or Tableau, wired to Anaplan/Adaptive.
3. **Schedule** governance cadence: monthly CFO flash, quarterly board pack, semi-annual VCP rebaseline, 18-month PAR (calendar invites out at close).
4. **Link** 30–40% of deal sponsor and CEO-of-target LTI to synergy capture + post-deal ROIC.
5. **Codify** a Course-Correction Decision Tree with thresholds (>10% variance → written remediation; >20% → IC escalation).
6. **Disclose** acquisition KPIs externally for 8 quarters if listed (IR alignment).
7. **Run** blameless post-mortem; update DD playbook and lessons-learned log in Confluence/Notion.
8. **Trigger** goodwill impairment test under IFRS 3 / ASC 805 on any red-flag event (not just annual).

## 9. Quick-Recall Card
- Monitor against the **deal model**, not the annual budget.
- Value scorecard = Strategic / Financial / Integration / People, RAG-coded.
- Cadence: monthly Yr 1, quarterly Yr 2–3, 18-month PAR mandatory.
- Trigger course correction at >10% variance; escalate at >20%.
- Post-deal ROIC < WACC = value destruction; test goodwill under IFRS 3 / ASC 805.
- As a PM/Consultant/AI Lead, the one question to answer with this framework is: "Is this acquisition still earning its cost of capital against the frozen deal model, and if not, what single course correction do we commit to this quarter?"
