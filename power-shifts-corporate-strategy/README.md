# Power Shifts and Corporate Strategy

This course explores how power operates within and around organizations, and how leaders can craft strategy in an uncertain, rapidly shifting world. It covers the foundations of power dynamics, geopolitical forces, technology-driven disruption, stakeholder influence, political strategy, platform economics, alliances, leadership transitions, governance, information asymmetry, negotiation, and resilience. The course includes 12 concept modules and 210 structured practice questions across three difficulty levels.
