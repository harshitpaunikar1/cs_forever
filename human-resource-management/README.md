# Human Resource Management

A structured guide to HRM fundamentals — from workforce planning and recruitment
through performance management, compensation, and employee development. Each topic
covers the core concept, key terms, real use cases, and worked examples, followed
by 210 practice questions graded Basic → Intermediate → Advanced.

---

## Core Topics

| Topic | What You Will Learn |
|-------|---------------------|
| Overview of HRM | What HRM is and why it matters in every organization |
| Strategic HRM | Aligning HR practices with business goals |
| HR Planning | Forecasting workforce needs and closing gaps |
| Job Analysis | Defining roles, responsibilities, and requirements |
| Recruitment | Attracting and sourcing the right candidates |
| Selection | Choosing the best fit through structured evaluation |
| Performance Management | Setting goals, measuring, and managing results |
| Performance Review & Feedback | Conducting appraisals and delivering effective feedback |
| Employee Development I | Learning basics, types of training, and L&D principles |
| Employee Development II | Training design, delivery models, and evaluation |
| Employee Engagement | Building motivation, commitment, and retention |
| Job Evaluation | Building fair and competitive pay structures |
| Compensation & Benefits | Designing total rewards that attract and retain talent |
| Employee Separation | Managing exits, layoffs, and terminations ethically |
| Influencing Employees | HR as a change and implementation partner |
