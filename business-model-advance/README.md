# Business Model Advance

This course covers advanced business model concepts — from network effects and platform design to hybrid models, organizational alignment, and rapid prototyping. It includes 12 concept files organized into three thematic areas plus 210 graded practice questions across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels.
