# Complex Problem Analysis & Effective Solutions

Interview question bank from `LLM_Azure_AI_Interview_700_QA.docx`.

## Questions

### 1. How do you approach a complex problem?

**Answer:** First understand the requirement, break it into parts, identify constraints, propose options, and test the best solution.

### 2. What is problem decomposition?

**Answer:** Breaking a big problem into smaller manageable problems.

### 3. Why clarify requirements first?

**Answer:** Because solving the wrong problemwastes time and creates wrong solutions.

### 4. What questions ask before solving?

**Answer:** What is the goal, user, input, output, constraints, success metric, and deadline?

### 5. What is root cause analysis?

**Answer:** Finding the real reason behind a probleminstead of only fixing symptoms.

### 6. What is 5 Whys technique?

**Answer:** Ask 'why' repeatedly until the root cause becomes clear.

### 7. What is hypothesis-driven debugging?

**Answer:** Create a possible explanation, test it, and accept or reject it based on evidence.

### 8. What is trade-off analysis?

**Answer:** Comparing options based on cost, speed,quality, risk, and complexity.

### 9. What is decision matrix?

**Answer:** A table that scores solution options against important criteria.

### 10. What is constraint?

**Answer:** A limit such as time, budget, technology, security, data, or performance.

### 11. What is assumption?

**Answer:** Something believed true without full proofyet.

### 12. Why document assumptions?

**Answer:** It makes hidden risks visible and easier to validate.

### 13. What is risk?

**Answer:** A possible future issue that can harm the project.

### 14. What is mitigation?

**Answer:** An action to reduce risk likelihood or impact.

### 15. What is priority?

**Answer:** The order in which problems or tasksshould be handled.

### 16. How prioritize issues?

**Answer:** Use business impact, urgency, customer effect, risk, and effort.

### 17. What is impact analysis?

**Answer:** Understanding what systems, users, or processes are affected by a change.

### 18. What is cost-benefit analysis?

**Answer:** Comparing expected value against effortand cost.

### 19. What is feasibility analysis?

**Answer:** Checking whether a solution is technically and practically possible.

### 20. What is scalability analysis?

**Answer:** Checking whether the system can handlegrowth.

### 21. What is bottleneck?

**Answer:** The slowest or most limiting part of a process/system.

### 22. How find bottlenecks?

**Answer:** Measure each step, check logs/metrics, and identify where time or failures concentrate.

### 23. What is observability?

**Answer:** Ability to understand system behaviorusing logs, metrics, and traces.

### 24. What is metric?

**Answer:** A numerical measurement such as latency,error rate, or throughput.

### 25. What is log?

**Answer:** A recorded event or message from an application.

### 26. What is trace?

**Answer:** A record of a request path across services.

### 27. What is debugging?

**Answer:** Finding and fixing errors in software.

### 28. How debug production issue?

**Answer:** Check impact, logs, recent changes, reproduce if possible, fix or rollback, and communicate status.

### 29. What is rollback?

**Answer:** Reverting to a previous stable version.

### 30. What is workaround?

**Answer:** A temporary solution to reduce impact until the root fix is ready.

### 31. What is permanent fix?

**Answer:** A fix that addresses the root cause and prevents recurrence.

### 32. What is SLA?

**Answer:** Service Level Agreement defines expected service performance or availability.

### 33. What is SLO?

**Answer:** Service Level Objective is a measurablereliability target.

### 34. What is error budget?

**Answer:** Allowed amount of failure while still meeting reliability goals.

### 35. What is failure mode?

**Answer:** A way in which a system can fail.

### 36. What is FMEA?

**Answer:** Failure Mode and Effects Analysis identifies possible failures and their impact.

### 37. What is edge case?

**Answer:** An unusual input or situation that may break normal logic.

### 38. Why test edge cases?

**Answer:** Real users and systems often behaveunexpectedly.

### 39. What is boundary condition?

**Answer:** A value at the edge of allowed range, like 0, max length, or limit.

### 40. What is validation?

**Answer:** Checking input/output against rules before using it.

### 41. What is data quality issue?

**Answer:** Missing, wrong, duplicate, inconsistent, oroutdated data.

### 42. How handle bad data?

**Answer:** Validate, clean, deduplicate, log, reject unsafe inputs, and communicate data requirements.

### 43. What is ambiguity in business problem?

**Answer:** The goal or requirement is unclear or hasmultiple meanings.

### 44. How handle ambiguity?

**Answer:** Ask clarifying questions, give examples, write assumptions, and confirm acceptance criteria.

### 45. What is MVP solution?

**Answer:** A simple useful solution that proves value before full investment.

### 46. Why prototype?

**Answer:** A prototype validates ideas quickly beforebuilding the full system.

### 47. What is proof of concept?

**Answer:** A small test to prove technical feasibility.

### 48. What is pilot?

**Answer:** A limited real-world rollout before full deployment.

### 49. What is iterative improvement?

**Answer:** Improving a solution step by step based on feedback and metrics.

### 50. What is feedback loop?

**Answer:** A process where results are measured andused to improve the next version.

### 51. What is stakeholder alignment?

**Answer:** Ensuring everyone agrees on goals, priorities, and expectations.

### 52. How communicate complex issue simply?

**Answer:** Explain the problem, impact, cause,options, recommendation, and next steps.

### 53. What is executive summary?

**Answer:** A short high-level summary for decision makers.

### 54. What is technical deep dive?

**Answer:** A detailed explanation for engineers or technical reviewers.

### 55. What is data-driven decision?

**Answer:** A decision based on evidence, metrics,and analysis rather than guesswork.

### 56. What if data is incomplete?

**Answer:** State uncertainty, use available evidence, make safe assumptions, and collect missing data.

### 57. What is benchmarking?

**Answer:** Comparing performance or quality againsta baseline or competitors.

### 58. What is baseline?

**Answer:** A starting measurement used for comparison.

### 59. What is optimization?

**Answer:** Improving performance, cost, quality, or efficiency.

### 60. When should you optimize?

**Answer:** After measuring and confirming the bottleneck or business need.

### 61. What is premature optimization?

**Answer:** Improving performance before knowing if itmatters.

### 62. What is complexity?

**Answer:** How difficult a system or solution is to understand, build, test, or maintain.

### 63. How reduce complexity?

**Answer:** Simplify design, split modules, remove unused features, and use clear interfaces.

### 64. What is maintainable solution?

**Answer:** A solution that future developers can understand, modify, and support.

### 65. What is resilient solution?

**Answer:** A solution that continues working or recovers gracefully after failures.

### 66. What is graceful degradation?

**Answer:** The system provides limited functionalityinstead of failing completely.

### 67. What is fallback?

**Answer:** A backup option when the main approach fails.

### 68. What is redundancy?

**Answer:** Extra components added so the system can survive failures.

### 69. What is high availability?

**Answer:** Designing systems to stay available with minimal downtime.

### 70. What is disaster recovery?

**Answer:** Plan and process to restore service aftermajor failure.

### 71. What is RTO?

**Answer:** Recovery Time Objective: maximum acceptable time to restore service.

### 72. What is RPO?

**Answer:** Recovery Point Objective: maximum acceptable data loss time window.

### 73. What is security risk analysis?

**Answer:** Identifying possible security threats andcontrols.

### 74. What is threat modeling?

**Answer:** Systematically identifying threats, attack paths, and mitigations.

### 75. What is privacy by design?

**Answer:** Building privacy protection into the systemfrom the start.

### 76. What is least privilege solution design?

**Answer:** Give users/services only the access they need.

### 77. What is data minimization?

**Answer:** Collect and process only the data necessary for the task.

### 78. What is explainability?

**Answer:** Ability to explain why a system producedan output or decision.

### 79. Why explainability matters in AI?

**Answer:** It builds trust and helps debug wrong or risky outputs.

### 80. What is fairness consideration?

**Answer:** Checking that a solution does not unfairlydisadvantage groups or users.

### 81. What is monitoring plan?

**Answer:** What metrics, alerts, logs, and reviews will track solution health.

### 82. What is alert fatigue?

**Answer:** Too many alerts cause people to ignore important ones.

### 83. How design useful alerts?

**Answer:** Alert only on actionable, important issueswith clear severity and runbooks.

### 84. What is runbook?

**Answer:** Step-by-step instructions for handling known operational issues.

### 85. What is knowledge base?

**Answer:** A collection of documented solutions,FAQs, and troubleshooting steps.

### 86. What is learning from failure?

**Answer:** Use incidents to improve systems, tests, monitoring, and process.

### 87. What is stakeholder communication during incident?

**Answer:** Regularly share impact, status, actions, ETA if known, and next update.

### 88. What is root cause vs contributing factor?

**Answer:** Root cause is main reason; contributingfactors helped the issue happen.

### 89. What is solution validation?

**Answer:** Testing whether the solution actually solves the problem and meets success criteria.

### 90. What is success metric?

**Answer:** A measurable outcome showing whetherthe solution worked.

### 91. What is acceptance test?

**Answer:** A test proving a requirement is satisfied.

### 92. What is post-implementation review?

**Answer:** Review after release to see what worked, what failed, and what to improve.

### 93. How solve an LLM hallucination problem?

**Answer:** Add RAG grounding, better retrieval, cite sources, evaluate faithfulness, and usefallback when context is insufficient.

### 94. How solve slow API response?

**Answer:** Measure latency, find bottleneck, cache, optimize queries, use async, scale resources, or simplify model calls.

### 95. How solve high cloud cost?

**Answer:** Analyze usage, choose smaller models/resources, cache, autoscale, set budgets, and remove waste.

### 96. How solve unclear interview scenario?

**Answer:** State assumptions, ask key clarifying questions, propose a structured approach, and explain trade-offs.

### 97. What is your final problem-solving formula?

**Answer:** Understand, break down, measure, choose options, implement safely, validate, monitor, and improve.

**Answer:** Explain the concept simply, give a practical example, and mention trade-offs or limitations.
