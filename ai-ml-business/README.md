# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning for Business

This course covers the foundations, techniques, and strategic applications of AI and ML in a business context. It includes 20 concept modules organized from foundational skills through advanced architectures to business applications and strategy, plus 210 graded practice questions across three difficulty levels.
