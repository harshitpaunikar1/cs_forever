# Business Forecasting

This course covers the essential methods and techniques used to predict future business outcomes, from basic time series approaches to advanced scenario and sensitivity analysis. It is structured as 12 concept files across three thematic areas followed by 210 graded practice questions spanning basic, intermediate, and advanced levels.
