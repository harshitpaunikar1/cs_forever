# FAQ

## What is this folder?

`NuclearEnergy` is a self-contained nuclear energy curriculum integrated for the local app and organized in the same pattern as `Computer_Science`.

## What makes it different from a generic nuclear engineering list?

It is centered on plant reality: reactor operations, thermal systems, chemistry, industrial controls, maintenance, OT data, and safety-critical engineering work.

## Is this only for reactor physicists?

No. It is meant for people targeting nuclear software, automation, embedded systems, plant I&C, digital operations, predictive maintenance, and India-sector entry paths.

## Where should I start?

Start with [README.md](README.md), then move through Foundation, Core Nuclear, Advanced, and Projects in order.

## Does this rely on video courses?

No. The resource strategy is intentionally text-first and documentation-heavy, with official course pages, manuals, lecture notes, and standards-oriented material.
