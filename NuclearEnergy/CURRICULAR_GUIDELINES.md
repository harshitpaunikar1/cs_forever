# Curricular Guidelines

This compatibility file stands in for upstream-style `CURRICULAR_GUIDELINES.md` references and defines the local structure used by this `NuclearEnergy` study guide.

## Intent

The curriculum is designed around three layers at once:

- how real nuclear plants actually operate
- which industrial tools and engineering workflows industry uses
- which text-based resources remain useful in 2026

## Practical Reading For This Local Copy

- Foundation builds plant intuition, control awareness, tooling habits, and safety mindset.
- Core Nuclear explains the operating systems of a real plant: core, cooling, steam cycle, I&C, chemistry, radiation, and electrical systems.
- Advanced extends into simulation, digitalization, predictive maintenance, OT cybersecurity, India-specific career paths, and 2026+ trends.
- Projects turn study into portfolio proof.
