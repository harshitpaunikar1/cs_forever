# IoT and Blockchain for Business

This course covers Internet of Things and blockchain technologies — how they work, how they integrate, and how businesses apply them to supply chains, finance, and operations. Each topic is covered in a dedicated concept file, and 210 graded practice questions span three difficulty levels.
