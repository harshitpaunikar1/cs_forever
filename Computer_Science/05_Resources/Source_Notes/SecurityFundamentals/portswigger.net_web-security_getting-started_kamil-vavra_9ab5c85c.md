# Content

Kamil is an Application Security Engineer from a small town in the Czech Republic. He got into hacking at around 14 years old, and has continued to hone his skills for the past 15 years. He's
passionate about the security community, and loves how much it's developed and grown in the time he's been involved in the industry.
"When I started hacking, I didn't even know how to speak English. I knew how to exploit XSS, SQLi, everything in the OWASP Top 10, but my English was so bad. After I got comfortable
with the language, when I could read books and blogs, it opened a whole new world for me."
