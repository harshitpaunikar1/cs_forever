### Mailing Lists

Please follow the netiquette when posting on FFmpeg mailing lists, especially
avoid [top-posting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top-posting) and
thread hijacking (that is, replying to a thread and changing the subject line to
something completely unrelated that was not being discussed within the original
thread) and please read the [Code of conduct](https://ffmpeg.org/community.html#Code-of-Conduct).

Configuring your mail client to break lines after 70 or so characters is a plus. If you attach files, avoid compressing small files, uncompressed is preferred. Also please try to avoid posting mails with overly long signatures or nonsense corporate disclaimers that claim the content of your mails is confidential.

Attached patches should not have application/octet-stream as mime-type, but
text/plain or even better text/x-diff or text/x-patch.

If you send us a patch, please make it some form of unified diff. We prefer
patches made using `git format-patch`

from the root of your FFmpeg
checkout or sent with `git send-email`

as shown in
[Using git to develop FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/git-howto.html). For additional
information and guidelines refer to the
[FFmpeg Developer Documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/developer.html).

Please read the [Mailing List FAQ](https://ffmpeg.org/mailing-list-faq.html) before sending a message.

### IRC Channels

FFmpeg has two official channels on the [Libera](https://libera.chat/)
IRC network. Both channels are open and unmoderated. Maintainers
have operator status, contributors with patches in FFmpeg
have voice in the channels.
[Web.Libera.Chat](https://web.libera.chat/?channels=#ffmpeg,#ffmpeg-devel) is a web
client for these channels.
Please read and follow the [Code of conduct](https://ffmpeg.org/community.html#Code-of-Conduct).

### Forums

FFmpeg offers no official forum. However, we collect links to FFmpeg-related forums
found in the world wide web. If you would like to see your forum added here, please
send an email to the
[ffmpeg-devel](https://lists.ffmpeg.org/mailman/listinfo/ffmpeg-devel/)
mailing list.

Also if you don't receive a reply on the forums, try our official mailing list or IRC channels. And most importantly, if you see questions from others on the forums or mailing list that you know the answer to, please help and reply. The forums are largely community supported and few official developers read them regularly.
