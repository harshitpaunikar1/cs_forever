# Digital Marketing Strategy

This course covers the full spectrum of digital marketing, from foundational concepts like the marketing mix and consumer behavior to advanced topics including AI-driven marketing, attribution models, and ROI optimization. It includes 20 concept modules organized into five thematic sections, plus 210 graded practice questions across three difficulty levels.
