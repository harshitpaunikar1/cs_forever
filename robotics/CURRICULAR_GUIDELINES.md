## Intent

This robotics roadmap is structured to prioritize the open-source tools and workflows that show up most often in robotics software jobs.

## Practical Reading For This Local Copy

- Foundation builds Linux, Git, Python, C++, ROS 2, URDF, and simulation fluency.
- Core Robotics covers middleware, navigation, manipulation, control, perception, and robotics algorithms.
- Advanced extends into embedded systems, industrial robotics, drones, deployment, and robot learning.
- Projects convert theory into buildable robotics systems work.
