# Microeconomics for Managers

A structured guide to microeconomics from a managerial perspective — from
demand and supply through pricing strategy, game theory, and market failure.
Each topic covers the core concept, key terms, real use cases, and worked
examples, followed by 210 practice questions graded Basic → Intermediate → Advanced.

---

## Core Topics

| Topic | What You Will Learn |
|-------|---------------------|
| Analyzing Economic Problems | Trade-offs, constrained optimization, and comparative statics |
| Demand, Supply & Equilibrium | How markets clear and how shifts move prices and quantities |
| Elasticity | Price, income, cross, and supply elasticity in decisions |
| Consumer Preferences & Utility | How consumers rank choices and measure satisfaction |
| Consumer Choice | Budget constraints, optimal bundles, revealed preference |
| Theory of Demand | Income/substitution effects, consumer surplus, market demand |
| Inputs & Production Functions | Isoquants, returns to scale, and technology choices |
| Costs & Cost Minimization | Opportunity cost, sunk cost, isocosts, and input demand |
| Cost Curves | Short-run vs long-run curves, economies of scope and experience |
| Perfect Competition | Equilibrium, producer surplus, and economic rent |
| Competitive Market Applications | Taxes, subsidies, price ceilings/floors, quotas, tariffs |
| Monopoly & Monopsony | Market power, markup logic, and welfare loss |
| Capturing Surplus | Price discrimination, bundling, tying, two-part tariffs |
| Market Structure & Competition | Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg, monopolistic competition |
| Game Theory & Strategic Behavior | Nash equilibrium, repeated games, sequential moves |
| Risk & Information | Expected utility, moral hazard, adverse selection, auctions |
| General Equilibrium Theory | Linking markets, efficiency, and welfare theorems |
| Externalities & Public Goods | Market failure, Coase theorem, and the free-rider problem |
