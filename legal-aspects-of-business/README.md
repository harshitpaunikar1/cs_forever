# Legal Aspects of Business

An India-focused guide to the laws that govern business — from contract formation
and breach through company law, intellectual property, consumer protection, and
cyber law. Each topic covers the core concept, key terms, real use cases, and
worked examples, followed by 205 practice questions graded Basic → Intermediate → Advanced.
