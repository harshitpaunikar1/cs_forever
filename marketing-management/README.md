# Marketing Management

A structured guide to marketing management — from the core marketing process through
segmentation, branding, pricing, channels, and communications. Each topic covers the
core concept, key terms, real use cases, and worked examples, followed by 419 practice
questions graded Basic → Intermediate → Advanced across two modules.

---

## Module I — Marketing Foundations & Strategy

| Topic | What You Will Learn |
|-------|---------------------|
| Marketing Fundamentals | Process, opportunity analysis, environment scanning, competitive dynamics |
| Core Marketing Concepts | Needs–wants–value, marketing orientations, holistic marketing, planning |
| Markets, Segmentation & Brands | B2C & B2B behaviour, STP, brand equity, brand positioning |
| Product, Price & Place | Product strategy, services, pricing, marketing channels, retailing |
| Communications, NPD & Global | IMC, mass & personal communications, NPD, global marketing |
